          How to play

          • Every day there's a new set of 20 lookalike pairs, the same for everyone.
          • Two photos appear side by side. Tap the one that matches the name, or press ← / →.
          • Not sure? Press Don't know (or ↓). It scores 0, while a wrong answer costs a point.
          • After each answer you'll see both names, whether they're edible, and how to tell them apart.
          • Wrong answers on pairs involving a deadly species show up as 💀 in your results.
          • Use the ⤢ button to enlarge a photo.
